Privacy and Personal Data Processing Policy (Android)

This policy explains how the BulkCreate for Android application processes personal data, in compliance with Colombian Statutory Law 1581 of 2012 and Decree 1377 of 2013 (compiled in Decree 1074 of 2015).

1. Data controller

BulkCreate for Android is an application that runs on the phone of whoever installs it, with no accounts and no servers. The data controller is the person or organization that uses the application on their phone: they decide what data to load, whom to send messages to and for what purpose. The developer of the application does not receive, host or access the data processed on any phone.

2. Scope and definitions

This policy applies to the personal data of the recipients contained in the Excel or CSV files imported into the application or chosen by its user from the phone's contacts. The application has no user accounts: it does not ask its user for a name, email or password. The terms data subject, processing, controller, processor, authorization and database have the meaning given in article 3 of Law 1581 of 2012.

3. Data processed

  • Name and mobile number of each row of the imported file or of each chosen contact. The number is normalized to the international E.164 format.
  • The personalized image with each recipient's name and the optional message sent to them.
  • The status of each delivery: pending, sent, skipped, no WhatsApp or failed, with its date.
  • The application's preferences (language, theme and sending options) and the date this policy was accepted.

Only the name of the imported file is stored. The file itself is not copied.

Phone contacts. If the person using the application taps "Add from contacts", Android asks them for permission to read contacts (only once; it can be revoked in Android settings). The application reads the contacts' names and numbers only to show them in the selection list, and stores only the name and number of the chosen contacts, as recipients of the batch. It does not store or send anywhere the address book, nor any other contact data (email, address, photo, etc.).

4. Purposes of processing

The data is processed only for these purposes:

  1. Generating personalized images with each recipient's name.
  2. Helping to send those images, with an optional message, to each recipient's mobile phone via WhatsApp.
  3. Showing the status of the deliveries and a summary per batch.

The data is not sold, not used for third-party advertising and not sent to analytics or telemetry services.

5. Where the data is stored and security measures

  • Everything is stored in the application's private storage on the phone: the database, the templates and the images of each batch. Other applications cannot read it. There is no developer server or cloud.
  • The application makes no backups: Android does not copy its data to the cloud or transfer it to another phone. Uninstalling the application deletes all of its data.
  • The application has no password of its own. The data is protected by the phone's screen lock, which the controller must keep enabled, together with Android's security updates.

6. Third parties and international transfer

  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms): the application opens WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business on the phone with the recipient's chat and the image attached, and the person using the application taps "Send". The number, the image and the message go through WhatsApp's servers, which may be outside Colombia, and Meta processes them under its own terms and privacy policy. This transfer is made by the controller with their own WhatsApp account.
  • Android share menu: if WhatsApp cannot be opened directly, the application offers Android's share menu, and the image is handed to the application the controller chooses.

Other than in these cases, the data is not shared with third parties, except by order of a competent authority.

7. Authorization

  • Whoever uses the application accepts this policy the first time they open it and every time its version changes. The application records that acceptance on the phone.
  • The controller must obtain the prior, express and informed authorization of each recipient before loading their data and sending them messages, and must be able to prove it if requested. BulkCreate does not collect that authorization on the controller's behalf.

8. Rights of data subjects

Under article 8 of Law 1581 of 2012, every data subject has the right to:

  1. Know, update and rectify their personal data.
  2. Request proof of the authorization they gave.
  3. Be informed of how their data has been used.
  4. File complaints with the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce (SIC) for violations of the law.
  5. Revoke the authorization and request the deletion of their data when the principles, rights and guarantees of the law are not respected.
  6. Access their personal data free of charge.

9. Procedure for queries, claims and requests

  • Recipients and any data subject must contact the person or organization that sent them the message, who is the controller (section 1). The developer has no access to their data and cannot handle those requests.
  • Queries: the controller answers them within 10 business days, extendable by 5 more business days stating the reason (article 14 of Law 1581).
  • Claims (correction, update, deletion or revocation): the controller answers them within 15 business days, extendable by 8 more business days stating the reason. If the claim is incomplete, the data subject is asked to complete it within 5 business days; if 2 months pass without a reply, the claim is considered withdrawn (article 15 of Law 1581).
  • If not satisfied with the answer, the data subject may go to the SIC after completing this procedure.

10. Data deletion

  • From the application, the recipients of a batch (names, numbers and images) or the whole batch can be deleted.
  • Uninstalling the application deletes all of its data from the phone.
  • Messages already sent remain in WhatsApp, on the recipient's phone and on Meta's servers, outside BulkCreate's control.

11. Data retention

Recipients' data is kept on the phone until the controller deletes the batch's recipients or the batch, or uninstalls the application. The controller must delete it when it is no longer needed for the purpose of the delivery.

12. Minors and sensitive data

BulkCreate is not designed to process sensitive data or data of children or adolescents. If the controller needs to do so, they must meet the special requirements of articles 5, 6 and 7 of Law 1581 of 2012.

13. Changes to this policy

Each change to this policy will have a new version and date. When the version changes, the application will ask for it to be accepted again before it can be used.

14. Effective date

This policy is effective as of November 17, 2026. Version 1.2.0 removes automatic sending through accessibility: the application no longer has an accessibility service and each delivery is confirmed by the person using it. When this version is installed, any saved sending mode, date of acceptance of the automatic sending risks and number of automatic sends of the day are deleted from the phone. Version 1.1.0 added the phone's contacts as a source of recipients.
